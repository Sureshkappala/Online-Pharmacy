Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about purchasing, prescription verification, and healthcare deliveries.

How does prescription verification work at Stackly? +

When you upload your prescription, our licensed clinical pharmacists review the prescribing physician's credentials, dosage instructions, expiration date, and cross-reference with your active medication profile for any harmful drug interactions before dispensing.

Are all products and medications authentic and licensed? +

Yes, 100%. Stackly procures directly from regulated pharmaceutical manufacturers and licensed national distributors. Every batch is barcode-tracked with verifiable lot numbers and tamper-evident security seals.

How are temperature-sensitive medicines shipped? +

Temperature-sensitive medications like insulin and probiotics are packed in insulated thermal boxes with pharmaceutical cold-gel refrigerants and real-time temperature tracking tags to ensure potency remains strictly uncompromised.

What is your delivery timeframe and shipping cost? +

Standard shipping takes 1–2 business days, with same-day emergency dispatch available in major metropolitan hubs. Orders over $35 qualify for free cold-chain shipping automatically!
