Patient Guidance

Medicine Safety & Storage Advice

Clinical guidelines from our pharmacy team to protect medication potency and family safety.

Original Packaging

Always retain medicines in their factory blister strips or amber vials with label and batch instructions intact.

Avoid Moisture & Heat

Bathrooms and kitchens expose tablets to humidity. Store in a dry, cool cabinet away from direct sunlight.

Track Expiration Dates

Never ingest expired antibiotics, eye drops, or liquid syrups. Stackly guarantees minimum 12+ months shelf life.
