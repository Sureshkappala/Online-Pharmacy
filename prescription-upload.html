What Makes a Valid Prescription?

Ensure your uploaded document includes all mandatory medical details to prevent verification delays.

1

Doctor's Stamp & License

Must clearly state prescribing physician's legal name, medical registration number, and hospital/clinic letterhead.

2

Patient Identity & Date

Patient full legal name and consultation date. In accordance with pharmacy laws, standard prescriptions are valid for up to 6 months.

3

Drug Name & Strength

Exact medicine name, active formulation strength (e.g. 500mg), administration route, and total duration or refill count.

4

Clear Legible Photo

Flat, well-lit image without cropped edges or shadows. PDF e-prescriptions generated by clinical portals are ideal.

Prescription Verification Questions

Everything you need to know about our pharmacist audit timelines and clinical policies.

How long does pharmacist verification take? +

Most prescriptions are audited within 15 to 30 minutes during standard operating hours. You will receive an immediate SMS and email notification upon approval.

Can I upload a prescription before placing an order? +

Yes! You can upload it in advance. Once verified, it will be saved to your User Dashboard profile, ready to attach to your cart items during checkout.

Is my medical information kept secure and confidential? +

Stackly complies fully with HIPAA and international data security standards. Prescriptions are encrypted at rest and accessible strictly by authorized clinical personnel.
