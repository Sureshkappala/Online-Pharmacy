About Stackly

Bridging professional clinical pharmacy care and door-to-door convenience with zero compromise on safety.

Our Founding Purpose

A Healthier Tomorrow Starts with Reliable Medicine Access

Founded with a vision to make authenticated pharmaceuticals and home diagnostic equipment transparently accessible, Stackly operates under the highest clinical standards of modern pharmaceutical dispensing.

We believe that purchasing medicines online should provide the exact same peace of mind, rigorous pharmacist counseling, and safety checks as your most trusted neighborhood pharmacy.

Clinical Standards

The Four Pillars of Stackly

How we protect patient well-being at every stage of the dispensing journey.

1. Authentic Sourcing

Every tablet, capsule, device, and liquid formulation is sourced directly from licensed pharmaceutical manufacturers.

2. Registered Pharmacists

Our audit team consists of licensed doctors of pharmacy (PharmD) who review prescriptions, check for allergies, and ensure dosage accuracy.

3. Cold-Chain Logistics

Insulin, biologics, probiotics, and sensitive medicines are dispatched with real-time temperature loggers in thermal insulated packaging.

4. Absolute Health Privacy

Full compliance with patient confidentiality and HIPAA standards. Medical records and uploaded prescriptions are never compromised.
